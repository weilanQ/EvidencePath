# Original upstream resources

Raw PrimeKG kg.csv and complete CIViC monthly TSV snapshots are not redistributed in this artifact. They are not required for the bundled frozen-ranking workflows. Frozen candidates, labels and diagnostic inputs are derived data in ../processed, not raw biomedical databases.

For original-resource identifiers, download locations, exact hashes and reconstruction boundaries, see ../../docs/notes/third_party_sources.md and ../../docs/notes/source_input_manifest.json. Do not replace the registered snapshots with current live database responses when attempting historical reconstruction. Full feature extraction and training are outside the supported one-command scope.
